Program 01 · The ones that fail most

Washers & dryers

Laundry appliances fail predictably — pumps, belts, bearings, heating elements and vents. We diagnose the actual cause rather than swapping the obvious part, and on dryers we check the vent, because a blocked one causes both the fault and a fire risk.

Program 02 · Before the food goes

Refrigerators & freezers

Many fridge faults are airflow, seals, defrost systems or controls rather than the compressor. We diagnose those first because they're repairable; sealed-system work requiring refrigerant handling is a different matter and we'll say plainly whether it's economic.

  • Residential and commercial
  • Quoted after diagnosis
  • Access and power

Program 03 · Elements, controls, leaks

Ovens, cooktops & dishwashers

Electric cooking appliances and dishwashers share the same common faults: elements, thermostats, control boards and door seals. Gas appliance connections are not our work — those go to a licensed gas contractor, and we'll tell you rather than attempt it.

Program 04 · Fitted properly

Installation & replacement

A badly installed appliance fails early: a washer that isn't level walks, a dryer vented into a wall cavity is a hazard, a dishwasher plumbed badly leaks quietly. We install to the manufacturer's instructions, test it, and take the old one away where arranged.
