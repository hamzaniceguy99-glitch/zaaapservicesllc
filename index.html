How we work

A Florida company that will tell you not to repair it

Zaaap Services LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. Insurance details are available on request.

Some appliances aren't worth fixing, and we'd rather charge a diagnostic fee and tell you that than sell a repair costing most of a replacement. Gas connections and electrical work beyond the appliance go to licensed contractors, and refrigerant work requiring EPA certification is only carried out where that certification is held.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What does a visit cost?

The diagnostic fee is stated when you book, and it's credited against the repair if you go ahead. No charge appears on arrival that you weren't told about.

Will you tell me if it's not worth repairing?

Yes, with the numbers. If a repair costs most of a replacement, you should know that before approving it.

Do you work on gas appliances?

Not the gas connection — that requires a licensed gas contractor. We'll diagnose the appliance and refer the gas work.

Do you carry parts?

Common parts, yes. Less common ones are ordered after diagnosis, and we'll tell you the lead time before you approve the repair.

Is there a warranty?

Labour warranty is stated on the invoice, and parts carry the manufacturer's or supplier's warranty.

Do you work with landlords?

Yes — agreed rates, tenant scheduling and photographic reports across multiple properties.

Something broken?

Tell us the appliance and the fault. We'll state the diagnostic fee and book a visit.
